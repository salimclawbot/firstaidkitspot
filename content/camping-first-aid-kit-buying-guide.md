## Who this guide is for

## Evidence boundaries

Camping evidence begins with the [National Park Service Ten Essentials guide](https://www.nps.gov/articles/10essentials.htm), which says a pre-made kit should be adapted to the trip and personal needs and that dated items need review. The [NPS trip-planning guide](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/healthandsafety/trip-planning-guide.htm) supplies the itinerary, conditions and trusted-contact frame. Neither source endorses a retail kit or publishes a universal camping inventory.
