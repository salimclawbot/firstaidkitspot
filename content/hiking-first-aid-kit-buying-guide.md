## Evidence boundaries

The hiking framework starts with [NPS Hike Smart](https://www.nps.gov/articles/hiking-safety.htm), which calls for a route plan, emergency plan, communication method and the Ten Essentials without relying on cellular coverage. The separate [Ten Essentials page](https://www.nps.gov/articles/10essentials.htm) says to modify a pre-made kit for the trip and check dated items. We keep those planning statements distinct from medical action.
